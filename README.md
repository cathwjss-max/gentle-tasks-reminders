# Margin: Your Mindful Moment

Create a mobile to do list app and reminders (birthdays and events) App Name is Margin. Mobile app with Responsive Design for tablet and laptop. Thefeel of the app is Minimal, Modern, Aesthetic, Clutter Free, Morandi inspired colours scientifically known to boost productivity with light beige or white heavy colors and medium charcoal text color, mix of soft monospace and sans serif typography. Background of the app is white, with Morandi secondary and supporting colors. Purpose:most of today's to do list and calendar apps out there have so much features that it becomes an overload and decision fatigue, Margin strips away unecessary features and uses science backed points to boost productivity and make it enjoyable and helps your mind become a clutter free space.Clutter free mind, remembers tasks, birthdays and events in 1 place with intuitive, user friendly, minimal design.
